Exact Placements, Measurements, and Notes

    Source & important rules

    Based on Pathfinder Uniform Guide (2025), NAD Insignia and Placement, PDF pages 5–8 (printed pages 10–13), with eligibility notes on subsequent pages.

    • One pin per pocket-flap location: A Baptismal; B PBE; C TLT or Master TLT; D TLT level star or Staff Service Star.
    • Earned level pins run from lowest to highest left-to-right in this front view; the highest is at the wearer's left. No gaps for skipped levels.
    • Advanced bars sit above the highest earned name strip. Good Conduct sits above the advanced bars.
    • Captain and scribe plates are centered on the wearer's right pocket (left side of this front view).
    • Use your actual conference/union patch and approved club crest. This preview does not authorize insignia or replace conference guidance.
