Exact Placements, Measurements, and Notes

    Source & important rules

    Based on the North American Division Adventurer uniform instructions. The originally supplied guide was unavailable; local rules may differ.

    • Class pins are placed on the left front pocket in ascending order, lowest toward the center of the shirt.
    • The Master Guide class strip is above the pocket only for an invested Master Guide. Staff Master Guides may wear the Master Guide pin and Adventurer star/chevron.
    • Adventurer awards and other club-related items may be displayed on the sash; Pathfinder honors, patches and pins should not be displayed on the Adventurer sash.
    • The staff service star and conference/union arrangement are additional options modeled on the Pathfinder page, not specified by the linked Adventurer instructions.
